Built around the loop

Loops without seams

Most ambient players seek back near the end of a file and hope you do not notice. Nuru wraps at an exact sample and blends the join, so a loop never clicks and never hiccups.

Nothing streams

Every sound is a local file, checked against its hash before it plays. Once a pack is on your machine a dropped connection cannot become a gap in the audio.

Mix and save

Layer as many soundscapes as you want and set each one's volume. Keep the combination as a named mix and it is one click away next time.

Sleep timer

Fade everything out after a set time, so falling asleep to it does not mean waking up to it.

Pick your output

Send Nuru to any output device on the machine and leave the rest of your audio where it is. The choice is remembered.

Light on your machine

Audio is decoded as it plays rather than held in memory, so a five minute bed and a five hour one cost the same.

Cozy Mode

A full screen room that reacts to what you are playing. Add rain and it rains. Add wind and it drives the rain sideways. Add snow on top and you get both, falling together.

Turn the thunder up and the storm takes over. Turn it down and it eases off. The room follows the whole mix rather than picking one sound and ignoring the rest.

Yours to arrange

Three themes, four tile sizes, and a grid that stays the size you chose whatever the window does. Pick which output device Nuru uses and leave the rest of your audio where it is. Small things, but you are going to be looking at this for hours.
