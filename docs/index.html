Every sound you want,
all at once.

Rain over a cafe over a distant train. Nuru mixes as many ambient sounds as you like, each one seamlessly looped and played from your own machine.

Download for Windows latest release What it does

Loops without seams

Most ambient players seek back near the end of a file and hope you do not notice. Nuru wraps at an exact sample and blends the join, so a loop never clicks and never hiccups.

Nothing streams

Every sound is a local file, checked before it plays. Once a pack is on your machine, a dropped connection cannot become a gap in the audio.

Mix and save

Layer as many soundscapes as you want, set each one's volume, and keep the combination as a named mix to come back to.

Cozy Mode

A full screen room that reacts to what you are playing. Add rain and it rains. Add wind and it drives. Add snow to the rain and you get both.

Sleep timer

Fade everything out after a set time, so falling asleep to it does not mean waking up to it.

Light on your machine

Audio is decoded as it plays rather than held in memory, so a five minute bed and a five hour one cost the same.
